# ACM GRIET Recruitments Portal

![ACM GRIET](https://img.shields.io/badge/ACM-GRIET-2797ff?style=for-the-badge)

Welcome to the official recruitments portal for the **ACM GRIET Student Chapter**. This platform serves as the digital gateway for students to join our dynamic community of tech enthusiasts, developers, and leaders.

## Overview
This repository houses the public-facing application for our recruitment process. It is designed to provide a seamless, accessible, and visually engaging experience for applicants submitting their details and choosing their desired domains within the chapter.

### Available Domains
Applicants apply to join the following core teams:
- **Brand & Media Team**
- **Documentation Team**
- **Event Management Team**
- **Graphic Team**
- **Logistics Team**
- **PR (Public Relations) Team**
- **Sponsorship Team**
- **Technical Team**
